Need help? Email r.kunaviriyasiri@gmail.com. Tell us your device, and what happened. We usually answer within a few days.

Common questions

I bought something and it didn't arrive. Open the shop in the game and tap Restore Purchases. If that doesn't help, email us with the date of the purchase.

How do I stop the ads between levels? Buy Remove Ads or the Cosy Bundle in the shop. Reward videos stay optional.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app? Yes. Progress is saved only on your device.

How do I change my ad choices? In the game, open Settings → Privacy choices.
