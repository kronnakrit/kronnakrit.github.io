Sugar Paws Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026 · Published by Ronnakrit Kunaviriyasiri

The short version. Sugar Paws has no accounts and we don't run a server. Your progress stays on your device. Ads are served through AppLovin MAX, purchases go through Apple or Google with help from RevenueCat, and Google Firebase tells us how the game is played and when it crashes. Those companies receive some device data to do their job, as explained below. You can change your ad choices at any time.

What stays on your device

Your levels, stars, coins, daily streak, game in progress and settings are saved only on your phone. We can't see them. Deleting the app deletes them. The daily reminder, if you turn it on, is scheduled on your phone and sends nothing to us.

What we collect

We, the makers of Sugar Paws, don't collect your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location, and we don't sell personal data. The game doesn't ask you to sign in.

Your ad choices

  • In the European Economic Area and the UK, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown or analytics starts. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • On iPhone, Apple asks whether the game may use your advertising identifier. If you say no, ads are not personalised using it. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings under Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).
  • Buying Remove Ads or joining Café Club turns off the ads that play between levels. Reward videos stay optional.

Children

Sugar Paws is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game and data was shared with the services above, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its use. Because we don't hold personal data ourselves, most requests are best made to AppLovin, Google or RevenueCat using the links above, but you can always write to us and we'll point you in the right direction or forward the request.

Security and retention

Data sent to the services above travels over encrypted connections. Each service keeps data according to its own policy. Game data on your device stays until you delete the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Important changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
