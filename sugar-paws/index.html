Common questions

I bought something and it didn't arrive. Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases. Coin packs that went through while the game was closed are added the next time you open it. If that doesn't help, email us with the date of the purchase.

How do I stop the ads between levels? Buy Remove Ads in the shop, or join Café Club. Reward videos stay optional.

How do I cancel Café Club? On iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android, open Google Play, tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Deleting the game does not cancel it.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app? Yes. Progress is saved only on your device. Purchases of Remove Ads and Café Club come back with Restore purchases; coins don't.

How do I change my ad choices? In the European Economic Area and the UK, open Settings → Privacy choices in the game. On iPhone you can also change tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
